# Destination Notes

## Destination Information

- **Name:** Kigali
- **Country:** Rwanda
- **Region:** East Africa
- **Destination Type:** City
- **Currency:** Rwandan Franc (RWF)
- **Languages:** Kinyarwanda, English, French

## Overview

Kigali is the capital and largest city of Rwanda. It is known for its clean environment, rolling hills, local markets, cultural experiences, and growing urban development.

## Best Time to Visit

- **Recommended Period:** June–September
- **Weather:** Generally drier conditions
- **Alternative Period:** December–February

## Transportation

### Getting Around

- **Motorcycle taxis:** Common and convenient.
- **Taxis:** Available throughout the city.
- **Ride-hailing:** Available in many areas.
- **Public transportation:** Buses connect major areas of the city.

## Food

### Local Foods to Try

- Brochettes
- Isombe
- Ugali
- Grilled fish
- Fresh tropical fruits

## Culture

Kigali offers opportunities to experience:

- Local markets
- Traditional food
- Music and dance
- Art
- Historical sites
- Neighborhood cultural experiences

## Important Travel Notes

- Keep important documents secure.
- Carry some local currency.
- Respect local customs and photography restrictions.
- Check current opening hours before visiting attractions.
