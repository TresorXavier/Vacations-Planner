# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is the capital of Rwanda?

**Answer:** Kigali is the capital and largest city of Rwanda.

- **Category:** General
- **Tags:** Kigali, capital, Rwanda

### What currency is used in Rwanda?

**Answer:** The official currency is the Rwandan franc (RWF).

- **Category:** Money
- **Tags:** currency, RWF, money

### What languages are spoken in Rwanda?

**Answer:** Kinyarwanda is the national language. English, French, and Swahili are also used in different contexts.

- **Category:** Language
- **Tags:** language, Kinyarwanda, English, French, Swahili

### What is Rwanda known for?

**Answer:** Rwanda is particularly known for its mountain gorillas, national parks, hilly landscapes, biodiversity, cultural heritage, and clean urban environments.

- **Category:** General
- **Tags:** gorillas, wildlife, tourism, culture

## Transportation

### How do I travel from Kigali to other cities?

**Answer:** Travelers can use intercity buses, private vehicles, taxis, or organized tours depending on their destination and preferences.

- **Category:** Transportation
- **Tags:** buses, Kigali, intercity

### Can I use motorcycle taxis in Rwanda?

**Answer:** Motorcycle taxis are widely used in Rwanda, particularly in urban areas. Travelers should use properly licensed services and follow applicable safety requirements.

- **Category:** Transportation
- **Tags:** motos, transport, Kigali

### Is it easy to travel around Rwanda?

**Answer:** Rwanda is relatively compact, but mountainous terrain and road conditions can make some journeys take longer than expected.

- **Category:** Transportation
- **Tags:** roads, travel-time, geography

## Wildlife

### Where can I see mountain gorillas?

**Answer:** Mountain gorilla trekking takes place in Volcanoes National Park in northern Rwanda.

- **Category:** Wildlife
- **Tags:** gorillas, Volcanoes National Park, trekking

### Where can I go on safari in Rwanda?

**Answer:** Akagera National Park is Rwanda's main savanna safari destination and is home to a variety of wildlife.

- **Category:** Wildlife
- **Tags:** safari, Akagera, wildlife

### Where can I see chimpanzees?

**Answer:** Nyungwe National Park is one of Rwanda's major destinations for chimpanzee experiences.

- **Category:** Wildlife
- **Tags:** chimpanzees, Nyungwe, rainforest

---

## Nature

### What is the best place to visit for hiking?

**Answer:** Rwanda offers hiking opportunities in areas such as Volcanoes National Park, Nyungwe, the Congo Nile Trail, and various mountainous regions.

- **Category:** Hiking
- **Tags:** hiking, mountains, nature

### Where is Lake Kivu?

**Answer:** Lake Kivu lies along Rwanda's western border and is surrounded by scenic hills and lakeside communities.

- **Category:** Geography
- **Tags:** Lake Kivu, Western Rwanda

### What are Rwanda's main national parks?

**Answer:** Rwanda has three major national parks: Volcanoes National Park, Akagera National Park, and Nyungwe National Park.

- **Category:** National Parks
- **Tags:** Volcanoes, Akagera, Nyungwe

---

## Culture

### What should I know about Rwandan culture?

**Answer:** Visitors should show respect for local customs, communities, cultural sites, and traditions. Learning a few basic Kinyarwanda greetings can also be appreciated.

- **Category:** Culture
- **Tags:** culture, etiquette, Kinyarwanda

### What local food should I try?

**Answer:** Travelers can explore foods such as brochettes, isombe, ugali, grilled fish, beans, plantains, and locally grown fruits.

- **Category:** Food
- **Tags:** food, cuisine, brochettes, isombe

### Can I visit local markets?

**Answer:** Yes. Markets in cities and towns across Rwanda can provide opportunities to experience local commerce, food, clothing, crafts, and everyday life.

- **Category:** Shopping
- **Tags:** markets, shopping, local-life

---

## Planning

### How many days do I need to visit Rwanda?

**Answer:** The ideal duration depends on the activities. A short trip can focus on Kigali, while a longer itinerary can combine Kigali with national parks, Lake Kivu, and cultural destinations.

- **Category:** Planning
- **Tags:** itinerary, duration, trip-planning

### What should I pack for Rwanda?

**Answer:** Packing depends on the region and activities, but useful items can include comfortable walking shoes, lightweight clothing, rain protection, sun protection, and appropriate clothing for outdoor activities.

- **Category:** Packing
- **Tags:** packing, hiking, weather

### What is the best time to visit Rwanda?

**Answer:** The best time depends on the activities and region. Travelers should consider seasonal rainfall, wildlife conditions, hiking conditions, and their preferred destinations.

- **Category:** Planning
- **Tags:** weather, seasons, planning
