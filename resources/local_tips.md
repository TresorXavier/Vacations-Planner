- **Locations:** Kigali and major towns

#### Public Buses
- **Category:** Transportation
- **Locations:** Nationwide
- **Tip:** Buses connect Kigali with many towns and provinces.

- **Tags:** buses, transport, budget-travel

#### Travel Between Provinces
- **Category:** Transportation
- **Locations:** Nationwide
- **Tip:** Plan intercity journeys in advance, particularly when visiting remote attractions.
- **Tags:** transport, road-trip, provinces

- **Category:** Money
- **Location:** Nationwide
- **Tip:** Keep some Rwandan francs available for smaller businesses, markets, transport, and rural areas.

#### Cards and Mobile Payments
- **Category:** Payments
